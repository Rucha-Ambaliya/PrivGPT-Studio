# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing 🚀  
To maintain quality and consistency, this repository follows **strict contribution standards**.

---

## ⚠️ Mandatory Rules (No Exceptions)

1. **Issue Templates are mandatory**
2. **Pull Request Templates are mandatory**
3. All sections in templates must be properly filled
4. Low-effort, incomplete, or non-template submissions will NOT be reviewed
5. Maintainers may close non-compliant issues/PRs without further notice

By contributing, you agree to strictly follow these rules.

---

## 📌 Issues

- Use the appropriate Issue Template
- Clearly describe the problem or feature
- Issues without templates will be marked invalid

---

## 📌 Pull Requests

- Use the official PR template
- Complete the checklist
- Follow coding, commit, and documentation standards

---

## 🚫 Enforcement Policy

- First violation → Warning + `needs-template` label
- No update → Issue/PR closed
- Repeated violations → Contributor may be blocked

---

## ✅ Final Note

Standards exist to protect maintainers’ time and ensure quality.
Please respect them.
